COMPANION LAB · CLUB TOOLS

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026 · Local development release 0.6.36

About this policy

This policy describes data handled by the Companion Lab - Club Tools Chrome extension and its connection to the Companion Lab application running on your computer. The project is maintained by GitHub user n0xz0r. Companion Lab is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Electronic Arts.

Information we handle

  • Companion Lab account and connection information: the associated account email shown in the extension, a pairing code, and a revocable pairing token used to connect to your local application.
  • Game and website content: owned player identifiers and attributes, ownership and location information, SBC storage, active squad, challenge requirements and progress, evolution information, and relevant in-game announcements read from the EA SPORTS FC Ultimate Team Web App.
  • Feature activity: synchronization status and timestamps, pending synchronization state, SBC calculation requests, and build outcomes used to display progress and history.
  • Optional imports: supported inventory files you explicitly select for import.

The extension does not send your EA password, EA authentication cookies or EA login tokens to Companion Lab. It does not collect general browsing history, keystroke logs, payment details, health information or precise location.

Why this information is used

Information is used to pair the extension, synchronize your club with your local application, display club and challenge information, calculate supported SBC drafts, prepare user-requested player selections, and report synchronization or build results. Synchronization may run in the background while the supported Web App is open and the extension is paired. SBC preparation is user-requested; challenges are not submitted automatically.

Where data is processed and stored

In this release, the extension sends its Companion Lab requests to a loopback service on the same computer. Inventory and feature history are processed and stored by your local Companion Lab application. The extension stores its pairing token and synchronization state in Chrome extension storage, with local storage access restricted to trusted extension contexts.

The extension has no developer-operated cloud collection endpoint and no advertising or analytics SDK. Using the EA Web App itself remains subject to EA's own practices. This policy does not replace the policies of EA, Chrome, or other services you use separately.

Sharing and limited use

We do not sell user data, use it for advertising, or use it for creditworthiness or lending decisions. Extension data is used for the user-facing club synchronization and SBC preparation features described above. The use of information by Companion Lab - Club Tools complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and your controls

The pairing token is retained until disconnected, revoked or cleared. Disconnecting through the extension revokes the connection and clears extension connection storage when the local application is reachable. You may also revoke connections from Companion Lab Settings. Uninstalling the extension does not delete data already stored in the separate local application.

Local club records and history remain until replaced, removed through the application's controls, or deleted with the local application's data. Companion Lab Settings includes data export and account deletion controls. Copies or backups you create remain under your control. To stop future extension processing, disconnect, disable or uninstall the extension.

This policy website

This page contains no analytics, advertising, tracking scripts or embedded third-party media. It is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information when serving this page under its Privacy Statement. Visiting this policy page does not upload your club inventory.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the publisher contact shown in the Chrome Web Store listing or open a general inquiry in the Companion Lab policy repository. GitHub issues are public: do not post passwords, pairing tokens, account exports or other private data.

Changes

This policy will be updated when data practices change, with a revised effective date. Material changes requiring consent will be disclosed before the new processing begins.
